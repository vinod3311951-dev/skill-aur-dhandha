## Evidence labels

Use only: VERIFIED FACT / YOUR CALCULATION / ESTIMATE-SCENARIO / GUIDANCE. Unknown evidence remains unknown; do not fabricate precision, sellers, prices, benefits, eligibility, growth rates or a comparison winner.

## Release safety boundaries

No account required for core use. No name, exact DOB, address, Aadhaar, PAN or bank details. No buyer-seller chat, commissions, transactions, application submission or benefit guarantee. Tax is not inferred from MSME/SME status. Current eligibility, documents, fees, deadlines and tax/compliance rules must be checked on official sources.
