# Future Careers & AI Learning — Evidence Note

Verified: 2026-09-18

## Scope and safety
The product must not present a deterministic ten-year salary ranking or promise that a career will be high-paying. It may present career areas with strong growth/earning potential to research, clearly labelled as directional evidence. Actual compensation varies by capability, experience, employer, industry, geography and demonstrated results.

## Future-of-jobs evidence
Source: World Economic Forum, *Future of Jobs Report 2025*.

Official report pages:
- https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/digest/
- https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/2-jobs-outlook/

Safe product claims:
- Employer survey evidence through 2030 identifies AI and big data, networks and cybersecurity, and technological literacy among the fastest-growing skill areas.
- Technology-related roles listed among fastest-growing jobs in percentage terms include Big Data Specialists, FinTech Engineers, AI and Machine Learning Specialists, and Software and Applications Developers.
- Green/energy-transition roles also appear among fast-growing areas.
- Education and care roles are expected to see significant growth in absolute employment.

Boundary: WEF evidence is global employer-survey evidence through 2030. It is not an India-specific salary forecast and should not be extrapolated into guaranteed 2036 pay or job outcomes.
