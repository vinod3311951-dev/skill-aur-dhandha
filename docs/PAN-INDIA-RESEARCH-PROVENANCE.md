# Pan-India Research Provenance Framework

Purpose: define the evidence standard behind every “Pan-India research” panel in Skill Aur Dhandha. This document does **not** convert national context into local certainty. State, district, city, crop, customer, platform and business-model differences must remain explicit.

## Universal evidence hierarchy

1. **Current Government / Regulator / Statutory Authority**
   - eligibility, registration, tax, food safety, agriculture-market, energy, training, labour and other regulated requirements;
   - exact source and verification date belong in `docs/OFFICIAL-SOURCE-REGISTER.md`.
2. **Recognised National / Sector Institution**
   - training, skills, market infrastructure, sector-development and industry-context material.
3. **Current Route / Platform Terms**
   - only for the exact marketplace/network/platform being discussed; terms must not be generalised to every route.
4. **Real Local Evidence**
   - quotations, observed customer demand, retailer/distributor terms, rent, wages, input prices, transport, power, packaging and other operating assumptions supplied or verified by the user.
5. **User Scenario**
   - calculations derived only from user-selected/user-entered assumptions; label as YOUR CALCULATION or ESTIMATE-SCENARIO.

Unknown evidence stays unknown. No fabricated growth %, seller quote, subsidy, approval, buyer, salary, profit, demand or “winner”.

## Evidence labels

- **VERIFIED FACT** — traceable to a current authoritative source.
- **YOUR CALCULATION** — arithmetic from user assumptions.
- **ESTIMATE-SCENARIO** — what-if result dependent on assumptions.
- **GUIDANCE** — practical decision framework or research prompt, not a verified factual claim about the user’s outcome.

## Category provenance matrix

### Food
Primary official anchors:
- FSSAI / FoSCoS for food-safety and licensing/registration routes.
- GST / Income Tax / Udyam where relevant to the chosen legal/business structure.

Research dimensions:
- customer type and repeat demand;
- raw material, wastage, packaging, utilities, rent and delivery;
- shelf life / food-safety process;
- route-to-market and current local competition.

Never infer a licence category, fee or registration outcome without current official verification.

### Retail
Primary official anchors:
- Udyam, GST, Income Tax and NSWS where relevant.
- ONDC only when the user is exploring an applicable digital-commerce route.

Research dimensions:
- footfall / customer type;
- inventory turn and dead-stock risk;
- supplier terms, working capital, rent and delivery;
- returns, digital discovery and repeat purchase.

No supplier or retailer relationship is guaranteed.

### Service / Skill
Primary official anchors:
- DGT / Skill India routes, SWAYAM, NCS and FutureSkills Prime where relevant.
- Sector-specific official authority when the service is regulated or safety-sensitive.
- MNRE / Ministry of Power sources for solar and EV-charging related opportunities.

Research dimensions:
- capability and proof of work;
- tools, consumables, travel and safety;
- customer acquisition, service area and repeat demand;
- training/certification gaps and current compliance.

No qualification, placement, customer or income guarantee.

### Manufacturing
Primary official anchors:
- MSME / Udyam, GST, Income Tax, NSWS and sector-specific authority where relevant.
- CPCB / State Pollution Control Board / waste rules for waste-processing models where applicable.

Research dimensions:
- machinery capacity and landed/setup cost;
- raw material availability and quality;
- power, labour, maintenance, space and safety;
- buyer specification, packaging, logistics and working capital.

Machine budget bands in the app are discovery filters, not live market prices.

### Agriculture / Farm Business
Primary official anchors:
- Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare.
- SFAC FPO information.
- e-NAM for supported mandi / commodity / trading information.
- APEDA AgriXchange for relevant agricultural / processed-food export research.

Research dimensions:
- crop/input suitability, seasonality and production risk;
- grading, packing, storage and transport;
- local market, retailer, FPO, processor, institutional and export routes;
- actual buyer specifications and payment cycle.

No buyer, procurement, price, eligibility or export order is guaranteed.

### Digital / Online
Primary official / institutional anchors:
- NCS / DGT / SWAYAM / FutureSkills Prime where skills or careers are involved.
- ONDC only when discussing its network specifically.
- RBI / SEBI / other competent financial regulator where regulated financial activity is implicated.

Research dimensions:
- proof of work and niche;
- tools, subscriptions, data/privacy and IP rights;
- acquisition channels, platform dependence and client terms;
- delivery capacity and repeat business.

Climate-finance content must stay within analysis/support unless the user or provider is appropriately authorised for regulated advice/activity.

### Home-based
Uses the official anchor of the actual underlying activity: food, tailoring/service, education, manufacturing, digital, beauty, etc.

Research dimensions:
- whether the activity is actually suitable/permitted at home;
- household/business separation;
- neighbour/building/local-body constraints where applicable;
- packaging, storage, hygiene/safety, customer privacy and delivery.

“Home-based” never means zero cost, automatically permitted or guaranteed profitable.

### Media, Education & Creator Economy
Primary anchors:
- DGT / SWAYAM / NCS / FutureSkills Prime for learning/career routes where applicable.
- Current platform terms for monetisation only when a specific platform is discussed.

Research dimensions:
- capability, portfolio/proof and audience/customer;
- original/licensed assets and rights;
- production cost, consistency, distribution and analytics;
- privacy, impersonation, reused-content and misleading-claim risk.

No platform monetisation or audience growth guarantee.

### Green Economy
Primary anchors:
- MNRE for solar.
- Ministry of Power plus state/DISCOM/local requirements for EV charging.
- CPCB / applicable waste-management authority for recycling/waste models.
- Appropriate finance regulator/institution for climate-finance activity.

Research dimensions:
- technical capability and safety;
- site, power, equipment, maintenance and utilisation;
- feedstock/offtake for recycling;
- regulated-scope boundaries for finance.

National guidance is not a substitute for current state/DISCOM/local requirements.

### Export / Value-added Products
Current retained opportunities:
- Moringa Powder
- Dry Banana Powder
- Spice Powders
- Beetroot Powder
- Biomass Briquettes

Primary anchors:
- APEDA AgriXchange for relevant agricultural/processed-food export-market research.
- Product-specific competent authority for exact export/regulatory requirements.
- Biomass briquettes must not be assumed to fall under an APEDA food/agri pathway without product-specific verification.

Research dimensions:
- exact product specification and testing;
- packaging, shelf life / moisture / quality;
- destination-country requirements;
- buyer specification, logistics, payment and commercial risk.

Affiliate or retailer links remain separate from official export guidance and require independent commercial verification.

## Localisation rule

“Pan-India” means the app can show national-level context and routes across India. It does **not** mean one answer is equally valid in every state, district, city, mandi, DISCOM, market or household. Any action-sensitive decision must be rechecked against the user’s actual geography and current authority/counterparty.

## Audit rule

Formal Audit 1 must sample every category above and confirm:
- every factual regulatory statement is sourceable;
- national claims are not presented as local guarantees;
- calculations are assumption-driven;
- official and affiliate/commercial routes are visually and logically separated;
- no stale destination is promoted as current authority;
- every external official destination has a corresponding entry in `docs/OFFICIAL-SOURCE-REGISTER.md`.

This framework closes the architecture/provenance layer; it does not replace Formal Audit 1’s factual spot-checks and live-link verification.
