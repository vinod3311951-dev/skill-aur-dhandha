# Export & Affiliate Opportunity Register

Status: architecture/content inventory for Factory X. No commercial affiliate relationship is asserted by this document.

## Product opportunities retained
- Moringa powder
- Dry banana powder
- Spice powders
- Beetroot powder
- Biomass briquettes / green fuel products

## Export pathway
Each opportunity must preserve:
1. Needs & Requirements.
2. Product quality, testing, packaging, traceability and buyer-specification checks.
3. Destination-country/current export-rule verification.
4. Official/current export research routes.
5. No guaranteed buyer, export order, price, margin or income.

## Retail / affiliate pathway
These opportunities are also future commercial surfaces for:
- retail-ready products where an authorised partner programme exists;
- packaging and sealing equipment;
- dehydration, grinding, sieving or processing equipment;
- testing / measurement tools;
- business software, logistics and other relevant operating tools.

Instamart or another retailer must not be represented as an affiliate partner unless a current authorised commercial programme, permitted link type and terms are independently verified.

All compensated links must be clearly labelled **Affiliate / Sponsored** before click. Compensation must not alter rankings, calculators, comparisons, eligibility guidance or official routes.

## Government / official separation
Government and official export links remain non-affiliate and visually separate. The platform does not sell farmer/business leads, broker transactions, handle payments or take transaction commissions.

## Factory X release gate
Before activating any commercial destination record:
- partner identity;
- approved destination URL/deep-link method;
- commercial rights/terms;
- category relevance;
- disclosure wording;
- verification date;
- expiry/recheck date;
- prohibited-claims check;
- provenance evidence.

This preserves monetisation optionality without weakening user trust or the zero-transaction platform boundary.
