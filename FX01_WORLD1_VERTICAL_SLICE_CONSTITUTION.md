# FX-01 — World 1 Final-Quality Vertical Slice Constitution

**Status:** FROZEN FOR THE FX-01 AUDIT-1 BUILD  
**Authority:** FX-01 master execution contract + authoritative enhanced Sarhad blueprint  
**Scope:** prove the production language before scaling art/audio treatment across all seven worlds.

## Product signature

**CALM OBSERVATION → FOCUSED DECISION → PRECISE ACTION → SATISFYING ACTIVATION**

The five-second market moment must be real gameplay:
beautiful fictional landscape → small mechanical objective → stable one-thumb aim → precision hit → visible world-system activation.

## World 1

**Name:** Glacier Reach  
**Visual role:** first-contact world and quality bar for the entire product.  
**Palette:** glacier cyan / pale ice / deep slate.  
**Existing binary scenic plate used during the vertical slice:** `/assets/worlds/frost-ridge.webp`.  
**Reason:** the existing scene library is retained while production presentation is upgraded; gameplay truth is unchanged.

### Required World-1 presentation
- layered 2D/2.5D depth feel from the scenic plate;
- restrained parallax/drift on standard devices;
- low-cost snow/ice-atmosphere motion;
- dark foreground/depth separation;
- premium vignette and objective readability;
- Reduced Motion and low-tier fallbacks;
- no decorative effect may change hit truth or hide the target.

## Captain Rudraa

Rudraa is the calm precision lead, not a generic combat avatar.
For the vertical slice:
- existing original project portrait remains the identity asset;
- home treatment must become more cinematic and recognisable;
- briefing treatment must use the portrait, not a letter placeholder;
- subtle breathing/scan-line life may be used when Reduced Motion is off;
- no real military insignia, unit identity or real weapon styling.

## Home screen

The first screen must answer within seconds:
1. Who? Captain Rudraa.
2. What? Precision puzzle missions.
3. What next? One dominant campaign CTA.

Required:
- World-1 scenic backdrop;
- strong title hierarchy;
- Rudraa integrated as character, not a detached settings-card portrait;
- visible campaign progress without clutter;
- one dominant Start/Continue Campaign action;
- secondary Mission Map / Scenic Archive only;
- no signup, ad, or modal interruption.

## Gameplay feel

Preserve the deterministic mission engine and all target coordinates/radii.
Improve only presentation:
- stable reticle;
- subtle scenic drift/parallax;
- premium focus treatment;
- stronger environmental activation;
- landscape mission layout where viewport permits;
- portrait/mobile fallback remains fully playable.

## Audio signature

World 1 establishes Sarhad's sonic identity:
- ~88 BPM atmospheric pulse inside the approved 82–94 BPM range;
- low, restrained bed while observing;
- airy ice harmonic layer;
- subtle amplitude pulse rather than constant loudness;
- impact feedback remains concise;
- music must never overpower gameplay cues;
- audio remains optional and gameplay-complete when muted.

No copied melodies, military themes, patriotic music, commercial recordings or recognizable motifs.

## Accessibility / performance

- Reduced Motion removes scenic drift and nonessential animation.
- Low-tier devices receive reduced motion density and DPR cap.
- Objective remains readable without colour.
- All essential text remains visible.
- Target geometry and timing remain untouched.
- Target 60 FPS where practical; do not lower the existing 30-FPS floor.

## Acceptance gate for this slice

The slice is technically eligible for founder review only when:
1. automated build succeeds;
2. existing functional QA remains green;
3. World-1 mission remains deterministic and completable;
4. home → briefing → mission → result flow remains intact;
5. save/progression remains intact;
6. reduced-effects path remains usable;
7. no uncaught/runtime/asset failures are introduced;
8. the new presentation is present on the tested build, not only in source.

This constitution does not authorize mass production of Worlds 2–7 yet.
