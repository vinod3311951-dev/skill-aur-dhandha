# FX-01 — Founder Combat & Interaction Amendment — 2026-10-01

**Status:** BINDING FOUNDER AMENDMENT  
**Authority:** Later explicit founder decision. Where this file conflicts with older FX-01 combat/mission wording, this file wins for the combat/mission layer only. All unrelated Sarhad locks remain in force.

## 1. Scope deliberately reopened

The founder has deliberately reopened **only** the combat / mission-engagement layer.

Preserve:
- Captain Rudraa;
- seven fictional worlds;
- Glacier Reach World-1 visual direction;
- one-thumb mobile-first play;
- Telescopic + Environmental playable views;
- Binocular observation mode;
- local-first PWA;
- accessibility, Reduced Motion, privacy and performance laws;
- no real countries, borders, armed forces, extremist/terrorist organisations, ethnic coding or real weapon models;
- no gore or graphic injury;
- deterministic QA, save/progression truth and AMIT FINAL release gates.

## 2. Consumer presentation

Remove consumer-visible practice-range / cardboard / test-target treatment.

Production scenes must use:
- live world graphics;
- animated environmental machinery/objectives;
- moving fictional hostile operatives where the mission calls for combat;
- moving protected civilians in protection scenarios;
- real spatial interference between civilians, hostile operatives and mechanical objectives;
- strong readable silhouettes at mobile size.

Hidden automated QA and deterministic hit tests remain mandatory.

## 3. Playable view system

Three user-friendly view tools:

1. **ENVIRONMENTAL VIEW**
   - wide, fully playable battlefield/environment view;
   - tap/direct positioning as appropriate;
   - intended for spatial awareness and civilian/hostile/objective reading.

2. **TELESCOPIC VIEW**
   - tighter precision aiming;
   - drag-to-aim;
   - fully playable;
   - must share the same authoritative hit truth as Environmental View.

3. **BINOCULARS**
   - observation/scouting tool;
   - one tap in / one tap back to aim;
   - used to inspect civilians, hostile movement and objective state;
   - cannot fire while binoculars are active;
   - must not alter hitboxes, scoring geometry or mission truth.

## 4. Loadout system — 10 fictional tools

The game must offer ten clearly differentiated **fictional** loadout choices, including a catapult-style option.

Current working catalogue:
1. Vector Needle
2. Pulse Carbine
3. Twin Relay
4. Arc Driver
5. Slate Heavy
6. Echo Repeater
7. Beacon Launcher
8. Prism Rifle
9. Rail Dart
10. Field Catapult

Rules:
- no real firearm names/models;
- loadout choice may change silhouette, handling feel, audio signature, impact presentation and animation;
- loadout choice must NOT secretly enlarge hitboxes, improve hit registration, weaken targets or create paid/fairness advantages;
- Field Catapult must feel meaningfully different in animation/audio, while still using the same authoritative gameplay truth.

## 5. Captain Rudraa survivability

Add a readable survivability system suitable for longer engagements:
- defensive armour;
- health;
- blast resistance / blast-protection feedback;
- first-aid recovery;
- clear damage state;
- clear recovery state;
- no graphic wounds;
- no gruesome death presentation.

Armour and first aid are gameplay resources, not a reason to obscure failure.

## 6. Longer combat engagement

The founder wants longer, more engaging combat sessions.

Implement through:
- multi-wave missions;
- numerous fictional hostile operatives;
- escalating mixed patterns;
- movement variation;
- different tactical roles/silhouettes;
- environmental threats;
- civilian-protection pressure;
- mechanical-objective combinations;
- armour/health/first-aid management;
- mission mastery and clear-wave progression.

Do **not** use real terrorist organisations, real extremist insignia, real ethnic/religious coding, or real-world conflict framing.

Use neutral terms in implementation such as:
- hostile operative;
- raider;
- saboteur;
- masked hostile;
- breach unit;
- fictional opposition.

## 7. Defeat feedback

When a fictional hostile operative is defeated:
- allow brief, non-graphic vocal reactions such as short `ah` / `oh` exclamations;
- keep reactions short and non-sadistic;
- no prolonged suffering audio;
- no gore;
- no realistic death close-ups.

The purpose is responsive game feel, not suffering simulation.

## 8. Civilian protection scoring

Protection missions require moving civilian characters.

A civilian hit must:
- be visibly and immediately penalised;
- reduce mission score by a fixed, clearly communicated amount;
- update the civilian-hit counter;
- produce distinct non-celebratory feedback;
- never reward the player;
- affect mastery/debrief appropriately.

The exact penalty must be deterministic and regression-tested.

## 9. Retention principle

Target long enough sessions through:
- escalating challenge;
- changing wave composition;
- mastery;
- discovery;
- loadout variety;
- strong audiovisual feedback;
- protection decisions;
- meaningful recovery/armour management.

Do not use manipulative dark-pattern compulsion, deceptive timers or coercive streak mechanics.

## 10. QA / acceptance

Before this amendment is considered implemented, automated tests must prove:
- Environmental View playable;
- Telescopic View playable;
- Binoculars one-tap enter/exit and no-fire state;
- ten loadouts exposed;
- Field Catapult present;
- loadout choice does not mutate authoritative hit geometry;
- civilian movement deterministic;
- civilian hit penalty deterministic and visible;
- armour/health/first-aid state correctness;
- blast damage/recovery correctness;
- wave completion and mission completion truth;
- no mission becomes impossible;
- Reduced Motion path remains complete;
- Android/WebKit regression remains green.
